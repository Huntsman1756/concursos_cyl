Note: CNO 7211 is explicitly rejected because it corresponds to `escayolistas` (plasterers), not automotive bodywork. The frozen seed `Reparador de carrocerías` is reconciled to the TodoFP `Reparador / reparadora de carrocerías.` and the BOE `Reparador de carrocerías.` output under the CNO 7232 classification.
