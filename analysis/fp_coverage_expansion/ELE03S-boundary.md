The BOE occupational-output inventory is used in source order. TodoFP independently shows the current profile and its professional exits; SEPE corroborates that the title's 2025 contracts include `Técnicos de reparación de equipos electrónicos y de comunicaciones` and `Técnicos de reparación de equipos electrónicos`, but those statistics are corroboration rather than classification evidence.

Preliminary evidence supports the 7531 electronic equipment repair boundary for all four outputs. The slot is `completed` because the frozen ranking seed `Técnico de mantenimiento electrónico` is reconciled to the TodoFP `Técnico de mantenimiento de equipos electrónicos.` and the BOE `Técnico de mantenimiento electrónico.` output under the same CNO 7531 classification.
