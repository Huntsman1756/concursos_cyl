## Deferred `EOC02M|7211` relation

The exact TodoFP output is retained as an official source artifact, but it does
not support a publishable CNO relation:

> Juntera / juntero de placa de yeso laminado.

The official classification evidence creates an ambiguity that cannot be
resolved by similarity to the word “yeso”:

- BOE, Real Decreto 1591/2010, identifies `7199 — Otros trabajadores de las
  obras estructurales de construcción no clasificados bajo otros epígrafes`
  and `7211 — Escayolistas` as different CNO groups.
  Source: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2010/11/26/1591
- INE, *CNO-11 Notas explicativas*, p. 249, lists the exact 7199 example
  `Colocadores de prefabricados ligeros (pladur)`.
- The same official INE artifact, pp. 249–250, defines 7211 as
  `Los escayolistas instalan, mantienen y reparan tabiques y enlucen muros y
  techos de edificios y los decoran con adornos o revestimientos de escayola
  en interiores y exteriores de estructuras.`
  Source: https://www.ine.es/daco/daco42/clasificaciones/cno11_notas.pdf

The TodoFP phrase describes joining a plasterboard product, while the INE
artifact explicitly places “pladur” prefabricated-lightweight installers in
7199 and defines 7211 around escayola finishing. No authoritative crosswalk
resolves whether this output belongs to 7199, 7211, or another boundary. The
conservative action is therefore to defer `EOC02M|7211`; this packet publishes
neither a 7199 alternative nor a 7211 relation. The decision uses no title-only
inference, offer text, or material-word similarity.

`EOC02M|3202` and `EOC02M|7212` are explicitly rejected; `EOC02M|7211` is
deferred pending an authoritative crosswalk. No offer record or title-only
inference is used.
