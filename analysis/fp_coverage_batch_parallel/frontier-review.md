# Frontier review — parallel batch 1

Review date: 2026-08-13

The NAN proposals are candidate-generation evidence only. This review applies a
fail-closed rule: a shared sector, generic activity, or similar seniority is not
enough to publish a training-to-occupation relationship.

## Rejected NAN candidates

- `SAN06S→3314`: dental hygiene is not clinical laboratory diagnosis.
- `SAN06S→3713`: oral-health education is not social education support.
- `HOT04S→9310`: a chef de partie is not a kitchen assistant.
- `ELE01S→7521`: a maintenance manager is not established by evidence as an
  electrical-equipment mechanic or repairer.
- `AFD02M→3723` for itinerary design or route-safety management: these are
  ancillary functions, not separate evidence of the CNO occupation.
- `IFC02B→3811`, `3812`, or `8202`: the sole TodoFP output only supports
  microcomputer-system installation assistance.
- All `COM01S` candidates: the proposed mappings substitute neighbouring sales,
  information, recreation, telemarketing, or data-entry occupations for
  marketing, advertising, public-relations, media, and survey roles.

## Requires exact official CNO evidence

- `SAN07S`: diagnostic imaging, radiodiagnosis, nuclear medicine, radiological
  protection, and radiology research roles are absent from the current curated
  catalog and need verification against the official INE CNO-11 notes.
- `SAN06S`: dental hygienist and oral-health educator.
- `COM01S`: marketing, advertising, public relations, media control, market
  research, fieldwork, survey supervision, census interviewing, and research
  data coding.

The accepted set may be incorporated without waiting for those catalog
expansions. Programs with unresolved outputs retain an explicit partial-coverage
boundary; unsupported relations remain unavailable.
