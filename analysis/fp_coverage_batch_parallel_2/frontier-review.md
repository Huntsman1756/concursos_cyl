## Rejected candidates and unresolved programs

- `SAN06S`: the curated catalog has no dental-hygienist or oral-health educator
  occupation. Codes 3314, 5611, 5612, 5621, 5629, 3713, and 2252 describe
  different clinical, care, social-education, or early-years roles.
- `COM01S`: all proposed codes replace marketing, advertising, public
  relations, media, market-research, survey, or fieldwork roles with adjacent
  sales, information, promotion, telemarketing, statistical-office, or
  data-entry occupations.
- `HOT01S→5831`: the lexical fit for hotel floor-service supervision is strong,
  but the occupation is explicitly `rejected` in the curated catalog and is
  therefore not publishable. Hotel reception, reservations, events, quality,
  and accommodation management also lack approved exact matches.
- `ADG01S→4309`: the occupation is `draft` and excluded from public resources.
  `ADG01S→4210` narrows general public administration to libraries and archives;
  `ADG01S→5891` is a rejected personal-care occupation.
- `SAN01M`: emergency coordination, emergency medical work, telecare, and
  patient transport are not generic information, hospital nursing-assistant,
  teleoperator, logistics, or personal-care roles.
- `HOT03S`: guides are not generic information clerks; tourist promotion is not
  retail sales promotion; event and tourism-consultancy roles have no approved
  exact catalog match.
- `AGA01B`: do not elevate TodoFP's `peón` outputs to qualified agricultural or
  horticultural workers (6110/6120). Flower delivery is not a transport and
  logistics clerk role, and floristry assistance is not horticultural work.

The accepted set can be incorporated as reviewed partial coverage. The three
programs with no accepted relationship remain pending until the curated catalog
contains an officially evidenced occupation.
