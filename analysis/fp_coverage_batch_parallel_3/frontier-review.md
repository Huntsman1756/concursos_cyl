# Frontier review — parallel batch 3

Review date: 2026-08-14

NAN proposals are research drafts, not publication authority. A relationship is
accepted only when the TodoFP output and an approved curated CNO-11 occupation
describe the same role or a direct functional equivalent. Sector similarity,
generic fallback categories, and changes of responsibility level are rejected.

## Rejected candidates and unresolved programs

- `EOC01S`: the approved catalog has no direct occupation for building-project
  drafting, BIM, energy certification, cost control, or technical document
  management. Generic engineering, archives, and construction trades do not
  preserve the functions or level.
- `SAN32`: dietetics, nutrition, food hygiene, health education, and catering
  food-management roles are absent. Clinical auxiliaries, cooks, and industrial
  quality-control technicians are different occupations.
- `INA01M`: bakery, pastry, confectionery, and related production roles are
  absent. Cooks and kitchen assistants are not direct equivalents.
- `IMS01M`: sound production, microphone operation, DJ/VJ, event lighting, and
  photographic retouching are absent. Electronics repair and software roles are
  functionally different.
- `FME01S→3209`: a generic manufacturing supervisor does not provide enough
  evidence for the three specific supervisor outputs. The CNC, production, and
  automated-systems programmer outputs also remain unmatched.
- `HOT01E→5110/9310`: cooks and kitchen assistants do not directly represent
  master bakers, pastry makers, dough producers, confectioners, or sensory
  tasters. All proposed matches were low-confidence sector substitutions.
- `AGA01S→6110/6120/8321/7812/3129`: the candidates either change an
  owner/coordinator/manager role into an operative one or cover only an adjacent
  activity. Forest, wildlife, rural surveillance, fire-prevention, habitat, and
  forestry-supervision roles remain absent.
- `QUI02M`: the worker produced provider traffic but did not complete the
  auditable worker contract after its runtime became orphaned. No candidate is
  accepted from that incomplete execution; it must be retried.

This review therefore adds one evidence-backed relationship and records six
programs as researched without a publishable match. `QUI02M` remains pending.
